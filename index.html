CMI Connect is a new offering by the Cause Marketing Initiative that aims to provide career-related programming to complement the pro-bono consulting work our students engage in early in the year.

For Phase 2 of CMI Connect, we are piloting a series of networking sessions with Northwestern alumni to support you all during your job search this fall. On this website, you may select up to 3 alumni you'd like to chat with. We will use these preferences, as well as the responses you shared in the career interest survey this summer, to match you with 0-2 other students and connect you all with the alum via email. Please note that these sessions are 100% student-led, so it will be up to you to schedule your coffee chats once connected.

To make this an inclusive experience, CMI Connect programming is open to all members of the IMC cohort regardless of whether you are official members of CMI.

How does matching actually work?
You pick up to 3 (for now) alumni, add them to your basket and submit your name and email address. We will use these preferences, as well as the responses you shared in the career interest survey this summer, to match you with 0-2 other students and connect you all with the alum via email. From there, it's up to you to coordinate the conversation.
Are these 1:1 coffee chats or group sessions?
It depends on how many people request the same alum! We will do our best to ensure any groups we form have no more than 3 students total.
What if my top pick is no longer available?
Ranking 3 gives us room to work with. We'll try your second or third choice. We will try our best to limit groups to no more than 3 students.
Is this affiliated with Career Services?
No. This is run independently by CMI. However, please feel free to reach out to Career Services for more targeted career advising!
I'm nervous about coffee chatting. What can I do?
Prof. Jacqueline Babb has very kindly offered to host mock 1:1 coffee chats to anyone that feels uneasy about networking. Please shoot her an email and she'll be happy to help!
I already submitted — how do I change my picks?
Use the "Edit your response" link from the confirmation email you got when you first submitted, rather than clicking "Submit my picks" again here. That way your changes update your original response instead of being blocked as a duplicate.
